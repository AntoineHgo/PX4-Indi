# Report figures

Generated by `indi_flight/bench/report_figs/make_all.sh` (host python3, matplotlib 3.11, Agg, vector PDF, serif font with
Computer Modern mathtext; Libertinus Serif is not installed here, so DejaVu Serif is the fallback). Shared style:
`bench/report_figs/style.py`. Output folder: `report/pdf/figures/`. Width 6.0 in (all figures). Test:
`python3 indi_flight/bench/report_figs/tests/test_figs.py` (12 tests, all OK). Paths below are relative to `indi_flight/`.

## Figures made

### 1. `g2_margins.pdf` (script `bench/report_figs/fig_g2_margins.py`)
- Data: `results/freeze10_s193/g2o/pt*_margins.json` (24 outer-loop cells, primary scorer), `results/freeze10_s193/g2s/ax2_r400_s1_margins.json` (attitude spot), `results/freeze10_s193/g2s/roll800_fwdef_s2/margins.txt` (rate spot).
- Shows: phase, gain and delay margin of every flown cell, grouped by loop (rate, attitude, velocity/position xy and z, acceleration xy and z), filled = 400 Hz, open = 800 Hz, against the thresholds PM 45 deg, GM 6 dB and the delay-margin floor (2 control periods at 800 and 400 Hz; telemetry latency not included, its value is not in the evidence).
- Caption: "Stability margins measured in flight at the frozen configuration. Each marker is one flown cell; dashed lines are the acceptance thresholds. Loops without a -180 deg crossing in the measured band have no gain margin. The delay margin is far above two control periods in every cell. The 250 Hz controller rate was not measured."
- Place: chapter 3, section 3.2 (G2); also the margin sections of chapters 1 and 2.
- Cross-checked (test): min outer PM 49.1 deg (`pt0_ax2_r400_s2`), min xy GM 13.5 dB, min delay margin 229 ms (`pt1_ax3_r800_s1`), 24 cells, `pt1_ax3_r800_s2` GM 22.4 dB, `pt1_ax1_r800_s1` PM 71.3 deg; rate spot GM 15.7 dB / PM 46.9 deg / DM 75.5 ms; attitude spot GM 13.7 dB at 4.29 Hz / PM 48.0 deg / DM 93.5 ms (the figure uses the Q13 A reading; the signed minimum -17.31 dB is checked but not drawn).

### 2. `g2_bode_rate.pdf` (script `fig_g2_bode_rate.py`)
- Data: `results/freeze10_s193/g2s/roll800_fwdef_s2/margins.txt` (18 measured bins: frequency, |L| in dB, phase).
- Shows: measured open-loop frequency response of the roll rate loop at 800 Hz: Bode (a) and Nichols (b), with the phase margin (46.9 deg at 1.72 Hz) and the gain margin (15.7 dB at 18.3 Hz) marked. Measured only: no linear-model overlay (see below).
- Caption: "Measured open-loop response L of the roll rate loop (800 Hz controller rate, multisine injection at the torque setpoint). (a) Gain and phase. (b) Nichols chart. The loop crosses 0 dB at 1.72 Hz with 46.9 deg of phase margin and reaches -180 deg at 18.3 Hz with 15.7 dB of gain margin."
- Place: chapter 1, section 8 (rate-loop margins) and chapter 3, section 3.2.
- Cross-checked: PM 46.9, GM 15.7 dB, DM 75.5 ms as in chapter 3; the test also checks that the phase at the interpolated 0 dB crossing reproduces the PM within 2 deg.

### 3. `step_attitude.pdf` (script `fig_steps.py`; traces `results/report_traces/step_*.csv`)
- Data: decimated traces from `freeze10_s193/steps/seed1/step_{roll,pitch,yaw}_indi/flight.ulg` (INDI, frozen configuration, seed 1) and `indi_vs_pid/step_{roll,pitch,yaw}_pid/flight.ulg` (PID reference); see `results/report_traces/README.md`.
- Shows: 15 deg roll, 15 deg pitch and 45 deg yaw attitude steps; angle (top) and body rate (bottom), INDI against the PID reference, 2 % band shaded.
- Caption: "Attitude step responses of the INDI controller and of the PID reference in simulated hover (15 deg roll and pitch, 45 deg yaw). Both settle inside the 2 % band within the acceptance limits (450 ms, yaw 1350 ms); INDI overshoots by less than 1 %, the PID reference barely overshoots." Values for the text (computed by the test path from the CSV, 5 ms grid): INDI overshoot 0.41 / 0.65 / 0.03 %, 2 % settling 345 / 335 / 1170 ms (roll / pitch / yaw); PID 0 / 0 / 0.24 %, 365 / 365 / 1210 ms.
- Place: chapter 3, section 3.2 (G2-STEP).
- Cross-checked: INDI overshoot and settling reproduce `results/freeze10_s193/steps/step_out.txt` (seed 1 rows) within 0.05 % and 10 ms; limits 5 % and 450 / 1350 ms; chapter values 0.65 % worst overshoot and 347.5 ms worst settling; PID reference within the derivation values quoted in the chapter (overshoot <= 0.3 %, settling <= 412 ms roll/pitch, <= 1240 ms yaw).
- Caveat: the PID flights are the paired flights of an earlier comparison (same airframe, simulator, 800 Hz, seed 1; the stock PID path is unchanged), not the flights that produced the 412 / 1240 ms derivation values.

### 4. `g3_campaign.pdf` (script `fig_g3_campaign.py`)
- Data: `results/g3_campaign_f10/SCORES.tsv`, `run_NNN/g3_checks.json` (steady-state horizontal RMS), `run_NNN/g3_draw.json` (drawn factors), 300 runs.
- Shows: (a) histogram of the per-run steady-state horizontal RMS (limit 0.3 m), pass and fail stacked; (b) the same metric against the drawn motor time-constant factor, the two runs aborted by loss of control marked on top; (c), (d) percentage of runs failing (failed on key 1, or aborted by loss of control) by bin of motor time constant and of added loop delay, run count above each bar.
- Caption: "Robustness campaign: 300 simulated flights with randomly drawn plant and disturbance factors. 218 runs pass, 79 fail at least one item and 2 end in loss of control (one further run was not scored). Failures are more frequent with slow motors and large added delay. The campaign is not passed."
- Place: chapter 3, section 3.3 (G3) and chapter 4, section 4.1.
- Cross-checked: 300 runs, 297 scored, 218 pass, 79 fail ("79 of 297"), 2 loss of control (runs 245 and 264, for source notes only), 94.95 % item 3 steady state (15 failures), ranges of drawn factors (motor time constant 0.5-3, delay 0-10 ms, mass 0.7-1.3).
- Note: the per-bin failure percentages are arithmetic on committed per-run files (not printed in the chapters). The steady-state RMS is item 3 of the gate, not the item that fails most often (item 2, oscillation).

### 7. `limits_failures.pdf` (script `fig_limits.py`; traces `results/report_traces/g3_draw*.csv`)
- Data: decimated traces from `g3_routeB_s239/ref_s241/{indi,pid}_d3/run_003/flight.ulg` (slow-motor draw) and `g3_refly_s199/{indi,pid}_264/run_264/flight.ulg` (saturation with a weak motor).
- Shows: (a) pitch rate of INDI and PID on the slow-motor draw, 8 s; (b) tilt and (c) pitch rate on the saturation draw after the fault, INDI against PID, 60 deg loss-of-control line.
- Caption: "The two robustness failures specific to INDI. (a) With motors 2.4 to 2.8 times slower than nominal, the INDI pitch rate oscillates at about 2 Hz; the PID reference on the same draw does not. (b), (c) After a thrust-saturating fault with one weak motor, INDI tilts past 60 deg and control is lost; PID, flown on the same draw, stays well below 60 deg."
- Place: chapter 4, section 4.1 (and chapter 3, section 3.3).
- Cross-checked (test): INDI spectral line at about 2 Hz (1.5-3 Hz search, 0.125 Hz resolution) and PID peak more than 30 dB lower in power (chapter: 2.00 Hz, about 30 dB); INDI tilt > 60 deg and PID < 60 deg. The plotted tilt is the estimated tilt over 3.3 s after the fault (PID maximum about 34 deg); the chapter's PID maximum of 40 deg is a ground-truth value over a longer window.
