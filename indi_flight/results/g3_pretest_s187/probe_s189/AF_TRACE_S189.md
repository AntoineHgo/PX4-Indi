## Conclusion
- a_f (0.2 m/s2 at 6.84 Hz) is an ARTEFACT: aliasing of rotor vibration (171 Hz) through the stage's 88.9 Hz sampling of the 267 Hz acceleration topic. Not real motion, not frame, not rotation-rate leakage. Real tilt-induced a is 0.02. The line frequency = alias of the rotor/vibration line, so it should move with hover rpm (run 10: 5.2 Hz, consistent in kind, NOT checked).
- Ground truth/EKF ax 0.3 at 7 Hz: x/y position truth amplitude 0.28 mm; the attitude says real a is 0.02, and acceleration h*alpha with h ~0.045-0.05 m (a_f/alpha) matches it numerically, but that came from the alias coincidence for a_f; for truth it is likely a lever-arm/reference-point effect of the sim pose or GNSS (UNVERIFIED, not needed for a_f).
- INDI-specific: yes. The a_f = R*lpf(f_b) path exists only in mc_indi_pos_control/indi_accel_control (stock PID thrust loop does not use measured acceleration; PID line same freq but 3.7x smaller). Feeding the stage a 267 Hz topic polled at 89 Hz with a 10 Hz LPF at 89 Hz leaves aliasing unfiltered. Explains why POS_LPF 5 only reduced the line ~30-45% (alias is attenuated only by the LPF gain at 6.84 Hz, not removed).
