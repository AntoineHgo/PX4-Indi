test(indi_rate_control): add tests for the ESC freshness, frozen window, reset and rpm bounds

- ESC sample freshness: boundary at the timeout, sample from the future,
  zero/negative/NaN timeout.
- Frozen rpm window: setFrozenWindow(1.0) trips at 1 s and not at 0.9 s,
  a negative window does not fall back to a working window.
- Saturated motor: a command pinned at 1.0 with a constant rpm stays valid
  (limit by design), a dip to 0.97 with a constant rpm is flagged.
- AccelControl reset: a lagging mean_u gives a first-cycle collective step
  equal to the gap, a NaN mean_u gives none.
- normalisedThrustFromRpm: tiny negative rpm rejected, tiny positive rpm
  gives the exact square.

Each test was seen failing with the behaviour broken once in the source and
passing after the revert.
