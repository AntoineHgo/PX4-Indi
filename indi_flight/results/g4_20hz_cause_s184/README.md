# G4 20 Hz line at nav_state 17->4 and 14->18 (s184, log analysis only)

Script: `line20.py <run dir> [pair ...]` (self-test: `line20.py --selftest`). Line RMS = Hann PSD of a 4 s window integrated over 19.5-21.0 Hz; floor = same-width average of 15-18.5 and 22.5-26 Hz. Windows start at c-3..c+3 (transition) and c+10..c+16 (steady). Topic rates: motors/torque/thrust/gyro/indi_rate_status 800 Hz; rates_setpoint 267; attitude_setpoint, indi_pos_status 89; trajectory_setpoint 40 (all above 2x20 Hz, OK).
Runs here: INDI hover_harsh_s1..s3, cruise_harsh_s1..s3; PID hover_harsh_s1,s2, cruise_harsh_s1,s2.

## Results (hover_harsh_s2, 17->4 at 37.19 s; the other 5 INDI runs agree)
- Line is present at the transition in: motors 0-3 (4.5-4.8e-4 each, floor 0.7e-4, steady c+10: 1.0e-4), torque_setpoint x and y (4.5-4.8e-4), NOT in torque z (1.5e-5, at floor), NOT in thrust z (6.8e-5, at floor). So it is roll/pitch differential, not common-mode, not yaw.
- rates_setpoint roll/pitch 1.6e-2 rad/s (yaw none); attitude_setpoint q_d[1], q_d[2] 7e-3 (thrust_body z none); indi_pos_status a_c x,y 9.4e-2 m/s^2 (z none), a_f x,y 7.5e-2; a_c_pre_inject == a_c (excitation injection is not the source).
- Physical vehicle too: vehicle_angular_velocity_groundtruth x/y 1.5e-3 rad/s (floor 1.7e-4), local_position_groundtruth accel x/y 1.2e-2 m/s^2, attitude_groundtruth q1,q2 6e-6. sensor_gyro x/y 1.5-1.6e-3. sensor_accel: no excess (1.5-2.2e-3 vs floor 1.6e-3). trajectory_setpoint acceleration: no 20 Hz (5e-5).
- PID ref, same transition and signals: motors 2.8-4.1e-4 (2 s windows, 4.0 vs INDI 5.3 max), gyro 0.8-1.0e-3, groundtruth rate 0.8-0.9e-3, groundtruth accel 7e-3, q_d[1] 3e-3. Same line, same onset, roughly 0.5-0.65x the INDI amplitude. So the 17->4 line is NOT INDI-specific; INDI amplifies it by ~1.5-1.9x.
- Transient: 2 s window line RMS of motor 0, INDI hover_s2, c-2..c+7: 1.1, 0.8, 4.2, 5.3, 2.7, 1.8, 2.9, 3.2, 2.4, 2.5 (e-4). Onset exactly at c (jump from 0.8 to 4.2e-4), peak c+1, decays to 2-3e-4 by c+3.. (the floor of that period is ~1e-4; steady c+10 window 1.1e-4). PID: 0.4, 0.7, 2.8, 3.9, 2.5, 3.1, 1.5, ... same shape. Cruise runs identical in shape (a_c x 9e-2 at c, 3e-2 at c+5).
- 14->18 (OFFBOARD->AUTO_LAND): in the 19.5-21 Hz band the motor line is 1.5-2.6e-4 and about equal to the local floor (1.4-2.2e-4) for INDI and PID; no onset at c in the 2 s time course (1.0-3.8e-4 all along). The guard flags there (5.0-5.2e-4 at 20.0-20.8 Hz) come from the +-2 Hz (4 Hz wide) integration and the peak/median-ratio test on broadband energy in the descent; I do not see a tone. The late-window jumps (c+7 and later: 9-19e-4) are touchdown, not the line. Not a tone at 14->18 is shown on the log for the 19.5-21 Hz band; why the guard integration gave 5e-4 there was not re-derived (guard window set differs: airborne-span clipped, starts at span origin).

## Answers
1. Lowest signal in the chain where the line shows: the physical vehicle roll/pitch rate (ground-truth angular velocity and acceleration, also sensor_gyro), at the same time as everything above it. Sensor_accel carries no line, so it is not a sensor-noise or accel-filter artefact (shown on the log). Above it every roll/pitch signal carries it (a_c xy, q_d, rates_sp, torque xy, motors differential); thrust and yaw carry none (shown on the log).
2. Transient, not sustained: onset at the transition, decays within about 3-4 s to near the floor (steady c+10 level 1.1e-4 at motor, 5x lower). Also in PID at the same time at 0.5-0.65x. 
3. Code: the only nav_state-coincident event in mc_indi_pos_control at 17->4 is the take-off setpoint stream end (trajectory_setpoint jerk goes to 0 and takeoff_status stops at c in hover_s2; see McIndiPosControl.cpp takeoff handling ~l.898-980); no stage reset (blend is held on ground only; IndiStageMode resets are on arm and pos-control entry, none at this edge); jerk feedforward goes to zero (jerk[0] std 0.77 before, 0 after). The 20 Hz line has no counterpart in trajectory_setpoint, so it is not the setpoint feeding it.

## Conclusion
Candidate cause (hypothesis): a physical 20 Hz roll/pitch ringing excited by the take-off to loiter handover (end of the climb command and its thrust/attitude change) in the vehicle plus rate-loop plant, present in both stacks, and the INDI rate loop with its higher closed-loop gain near 20 Hz gives about 1.5-1.9x the PID amplitude. Not tested here: which actuator-side element (rotor dynamics in the sim, ESC model) rings at 20 Hz; a step-response on a bench log would settle it. The 14->18 failures look like a guard-metric effect on broadband descent energy (matches Q20), not a transition tone.
Caveats: per-run numbers in 2 s windows are noisy (+-1e-4); only hover_s2 was inspected at the full signal list, the other runs only motors, torque xy/z, thrust z, rates roll, q_d1, a_c x/z, a_f x, gyro, accel.
