# 89 Hz line, cause search (S180, analysis only, no firmware change)
Time base: log time of the scorer (actuator_motors first sample = 0). Window 36-53.5 s lemniscate; hover 6-20 s (same flight, 30 s arm, hover/hold before offboard).
Metric: sine-RMS of the peak bin in 87-91 Hz (Hann, 0.057 Hz bins), and ratio to the median bin RMS at 75-85/93-103 Hz. This is NOT the +-2 Hz scorer metric (scripts: sub_nonoise/narrow2.py, l89.py).

## Logged rates (F2 flight)
800 Hz: sensor_gyro, sensor_accel, vehicle_angular_velocity, indi_rate_status, vehicle_torque_setpoint, actuator_motors, esc_status (781/714 Hz).
266.7 Hz: sensor_combined, vehicle_acceleration, vehicle_rates_setpoint (Nyquist 133, ok).
88.89 Hz (dt 11.25 ms = 3 x 3.75 ms): indi_pos_status, vehicle_attitude_setpoint, indi_rate_feedforward, vehicle_local_position -> NOT RESOLVABLE at 89 Hz (Nyquist 44).
vehicle_thrust_setpoint: not logged here as 800 Hz topic with a line (collective only).

## Conclusion
Not sensor noise, not gyro/accel path, not ESC telemetry (esc line is downstream and weak), not 250/400 Hz aliasing. Deterministic line at the 88.89 Hz outer-loop (mc_indi_pos_control / attitude setpoint) update rate, present only while moving, entering at vehicle_rates_setpoint (attitude setpoint step per 11.25 ms through the attitude P/feedforward at 266 Hz), amplified by the rate_sp_dot lead into nu and torque. It is then a real 89 Hz vehicle rate (7.9e-5 rad/s).
Firmware decision it points at: the outer-loop output (attitude setpoint q_d / omega_ff) is a 88.9 Hz zero-order-hold staircase into a 266/800 Hz inner path; smooth or interpolate it, or reduce the rate_sp_dot lead on the held steps. Not proven: which of q_d vs omega_ff steps dominates (attitude_setpoint is logged at 88.9 Hz, unresolvable), and no flight changed the outer-loop rate to confirm the frequency tracks it.
