## Reading

- Correction to STATE s30, which read c00 as the best: three candidates score below the defaults. The best,
  c23, is 4 % below c00 (J 0.967 vs 1.007).
- Most of that margin is the hover term. The hover ratio of the defaults against their own reference is
  0.86, so the hover term is noisy, as in v1 (D-hovernoise). On the step scenarios c23 is better on
  step_roll (0.82) and rate_step_roll (0.92), equal on step_pitch and worse on step_yaw (1.09).
- With 2 seeds a 4 % gap is not significant. As in v1, round 2 (16 candidates x 3 seeds, span 1.5) decides.
  Round 2 is centred on c23 (`pid/r1/best.json`), not on the defaults: the plan for r2 was "centred on the r1
  best", and that is c23. Candidate 0 of r2 re-flies c23 on 3 seeds.
- Decision rule for the v2 PID baseline: same as v1. If no r2 candidate beats the re-flown defaults by more
  than the seed spread, the defaults stay the tuned baseline. Otherwise the r2 best becomes the tuned v2 PID
  baseline and INDI v2 is compared against it as well as against the defaults.
