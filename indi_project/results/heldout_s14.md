## Reading

- Disturbance rejection (com_shift, gust_diag, gust_x, payload_add): INDI attitude RMS 0.15-0.75x PID on every
  scenario, lower on 5/5 seeds each, with horizontal position RMS 0.53-0.94x PID and equal motor-rate effort except
  gust_diag (1.18x). Same mechanism as T8: the disturbance torque appears in the measured angular acceleration and is
  cancelled within one increment, while PID waits for its integrator. com_shift is the largest gain (roll 0.12x).
- step_diag (H6, diagonal attitude steps): INDI roll/pitch RMS 0.88/0.95x PID with 0.93x effort, but yaw RMS 1.30x
  PID (0/5 seeds). Position metrics there are meaningless (attitude-mode steps drift tens of metres, both families).
- mismatch_inertia (T9): INDI attitude 0.88x PID, pitch 0.42x, yaw 0.64x, at 1.26x motor-rate effort (0/5 seeds lower).
- Yaw remains INDI's weak axis whenever yaw is commanded (step_diag 1.30x, T8 1.14x, tuning step_yaw 1.10x) and
  slightly worse on payload_add (1.16x, absolute 0.03 deg, negligible); it is better on all disturbance-only yaw rows
  except payload_add. Consistent with the structural yaw result of `tuning/r4.md` (D-yawsp).
- Statistics: with n=5 paired seeds, the smallest two-sided Wilcoxon p-value is 0.0625, so 5/5 is the strongest
  result this batch can give; the acceptance test of `bench/scenarios.md` must be applied with that in mind
  The spec (`bench/scenarios.md` l.116) requires 10 paired seeds with p < 0.05: seeds 6-10 launched in session 14 (`results/heldout_s14.log`).

## 10-seed update (session 14)

All 120 flights are complete with 0 failures. Primary metric is attitude rotation RMS. The test is an exact two-sided paired Wilcoxon (`bench/summarize_seeds.py`).

- INDI is lower on 10/10 seeds with p = 0.002 in every one of the 6 scenarios. The median improvements are com_shift 84 %, gust_diag 44 %, gust_x 40 %, payload_add 27 %, mismatch_inertia 13 % and step_diag 10 %. The step_diag value is exactly the 10 % threshold of criterion 2, so it is borderline.
- Held-out scenarios flown so far are H2 gust_diag, H3 com_shift and H6 step_diag. All three meet criterion 2, with H6 at the threshold. H1, H4 and H5 are not flown yet, so acceptance (at least 4 of 6 H scenarios) cannot be decided.
- Yaw is significantly worse on step_diag: 1.31x PID, p = 0.002. That is a secondary metric, so it does not count against criterion 3, which is evaluated on the primary metric.
- Effort is significantly higher on gust_diag (1.13x) and mismatch_inertia (1.27x), both p = 0.002. Both stay under the 1.5x limit, but criterion 4 is defined on T1 hover.
